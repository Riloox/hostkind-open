# Command-line parameters

![Desktop version](/images/Desktop_only.png?8fb4d9)

**Command-line parameters** are pieces of information provided to *[Terraria](/wiki/Terraria "Terraria")* when it is started. They influence various parts of the game.

## Contents

## Client

The following command-line parameters are available for the standard *Terraria* application:

`-savedirectory`
`-logfile`
`-minidump`
`-logerrors`
`-fulldump`
`-disableannouncementbox`
`-announcementboxrange`
`-forcepriority`
`-localsteamid`
`-localwegameid`
`-ip`
`-j`
`-join`
`-pass`
`-password`
`-host`
`-loadlib`
`-p`
`-port`
`-steam`
`-lobby`
`-friendsoffriends`
`+connect_lobby`

## Server

The following command-line parameters are available for *Terraria's* [server](/wiki/Server "Server") application, `TerrariaServer`:

`TerrariaServer`
`-config <file path>`
`-port <number>`
`-players <number> / -maxplayers <number>`
`-pass <password> / -password <password>`
`-motd <text>`
`-world <file path>`
`-autocreate <number>`
`-banlist <file path>`
`-worldname <world name>`
`-secure`
`-noupnp`
`-steam`
`-lobby friends / -lobby private`
`-ip <ip address>`
`-forcepriority <priority>`
`-disableannouncementbox`
`-announcementboxrange <number>`
`-seed <seed>`
